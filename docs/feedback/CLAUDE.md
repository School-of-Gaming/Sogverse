# docs/feedback

Input from outside the repo: a tester's session, a stakeholder's requests, user
research. Things to *consider*, not things to do — more descriptive than a `TODO.md`
item, and not yet a commitment to anything.

- **Point-in-time and sourced.** Name who it came from and when; one doc per
  round/source. Findings are one person's view — worth reading as such, not as settled
  requirements.
- **Record the triage next to each finding**: what was done, what turned out narrower
  than reported, what wasn't exercised. A finding acted on graduates — into a fix, a
  plan, or a `TODO.md` item with the owner's approval — and the graduation is noted in
  place rather than the finding deleted, so the doc stays an honest account of the round.
- When every finding is triaged and nothing left is worth considering, the doc either
  distills into a `../records/` lesson or is deleted.
