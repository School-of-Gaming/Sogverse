# Brand Guidebook deviations

**Status:** transitional queue, opened 2026-08-31. Every entry here is unresolved by
definition — a resolved one is deleted, not marked.

The **School of Gaming Brand Voice & Identity Guidebook v2.0** (Jul 21, 2026) is the
definitive brand authority: where the app diverges without a recorded ruling, the Guidebook
wins. This file is the queue of the places we have ruled otherwise, or asked the Guidebook's
author to rule, so that a divergence is a decision somebody can read rather than drift
somebody has to guess at.

**Lifecycle.** An entry is **removed** once its ruling is approved and codified in its
permanent home — the Guidebook's own decision log, or a rule in a repo `CLAUDE.md`. The file
is **deleted once empty.** Nothing is ticked off, marked done, or kept as an archive: git
history is the record, and a queue that keeps its cleared entries stops being a queue.

**The four rulings.** *Expansion* — a rule we hold that the Guidebook does not yet state,
proposed for upstreaming. *Exception* — a Guidebook rule we deliberately do not follow in one
scoped place. *Rejection* — a Guidebook rule we do not follow at all. *Escalation* — a
question put to the Guidebook's author, where nothing changes until it is answered.
