## 10.1 Homepage hero · Level 1

> Children aged 7 to 15 build Human Skills like creativity, empathy, collaboration, and critical thinking, guided by professional Game Educators in small groups. Lessons run 90 minutes, once a week or more, from €59 per month.
>
> [**See this week's schedule**](http://url)
>
> *We call the world our gamers play in Sogverse. They will explain it to you far better than we can.*

*Why this works: the headline says what it is in seven plain words. The subhead names four of the seven Human Skills rather than claiming all of them, and carries age, supervision, duration, and price before a single lore term appears. The one lore term arrives last, in italics, as an invitation rather than a hurdle, and it hands the explaining over to the child, which is both true and disarming.*

## 10.8 Message to a parent whose child was targeted by toxic behavior · Level 0

> **Subject:** About Leo's lesson today, Tue, Jul 21, 2026
>
> Hello Petri,
>
> I am writing about something that happened during Leo's lesson this afternoon, and I would rather you heard it from me today than from him tonight without context.
>
> At around 5:15 PM, another participant sent Leo a series of messages in the group chat that were personal and hostile. They were about his voice and about his skill level, and they continued after he asked the sender to stop.
>
> Sanna Virtanen, Leo's Gedu, saw the messages within about two minutes, removed the sender from the channel, and moved Leo into a private voice channel with her for the rest of the session. She stayed with him until the lesson ended. Leo was steady by the end and told her he wants to come on Thu as usual.
>
> What has happened since: the other participant's access is suspended while we review it, and we have kept a complete record of the messages. I can send you that record if you want to see it, and I will send it without you having to ask twice.
>
> What I would suggest tonight: let him tell you about it in his own time rather than opening with it. He handled himself well and he knows it, and that is worth more than anything I can say.
>
> I would like to speak with you directly. I am available until 6PM today and any time on Wed, Jul 22, 2026. Call me on the number below or tell me a time and I will call you.
>
> Mikko Laine School of Gaming

*Why this works: not one lore term, in a brand built entirely on lore. The word "Toxie" would have been available and it would have been a catastrophe. Named Gedu, named sender of the message, exact time, chronological account, action already taken, evidence offered before it is requested, one piece of practical advice, and a direct line to a human with specific availability. This is the standard every hard-moment message is measured against.*

## 10.9 Website safety section for parents · Level 1

> ## How we keep this safe

> Not by watching your child more closely. By knowing almost nothing about them.
>
> Your child has a Display Name and an age bracket. That is their entire file. We hold no real name, no email address, and no date of birth for any gamer on the platform, which means there is nothing about them to lose, leak, or sell.
>
> The rest is structural rather than promised:
>

> - **No private messages.** There are no one-to-one chats and no unmoderated corners. All communication is in groups, and a Game Educator is present in every session.
> - **No strangers.** Only registered gamers and vetted Game Educators can be in the space. There are no outside invites and no friend-of-a-friend access.
> - **No profiling.** We do not build advertising profiles of children, and we use none of the techniques designed to keep them playing longer than they meant to.
> - **Your details stay yours.** Your child's Gedu can message you through the platform about how they are getting on, and cannot see your email address or your phone number.

>
> When something does go wrong, and occasionally it does, it usually ends the way it should: two gamers, a Gedu who saw what happened, a short conversation, and back to the build. If it needs to reach you, it reaches you.
>
> > "We keep the data minimal so the safety can be maximal." — Mikko Perälä, Princi-Pal, School of Gaming
>
> [**Read the full Safety Standard**](http://url)

*Why this works: the headline sentence is counterintuitive, which buys attention, and every claim underneath is checkable. Nothing here says "we take safety seriously". Note also that Discord is never named. The absence of DMs, strangers, and profiling makes the comparison without us having to pick a fight.*
