## Consequences worth noticing

Three of these rulings have effects beyond the line item, and they are the ones most likely to cause trouble if they go unread.

**Sogo has no defined rate, and that is a constraint on the store, not a detail.** Until a rate exists, store copy may say what Sogo buys but must never imply how it is earned or at what speed. "340 Sogo" is fine. "Just three lessons away" is not, because we cannot currently make that true. Section 8.6 has been written to hold under this constraint.

**Section 10.10 now contains an aspirational example.** The Tuomas post describes a former gamer entering Level 1 Pathfinder. No gamer has reached the Gedu Path yet, so **that post must not be published as written.** It stays in the guide as a model of how the story should be told on the day it becomes true. Until then, the loop is described as a path that exists and is open, never as one people are already walking.

**Human Skills gives Level 1 copy something it was missing.** Until now, plain-language copy to parents and institutions had no vocabulary of its own: it could only describe the Yty-Elements from outside, or fall back on borrowed education-sector language. Human Skills is the translated register in its own right, and the seven map cleanly onto the four. Section 5.2 carries the mapping. Expect partner, press, and website copy to change more than anything else as a result.

**"Prices vary" is now a formatting rule, not a disclaimer.** Because Gedus will set their own prices, any single figure in marketing becomes a promise we cannot keep at checkout. "From €59 per month" is the standing construction.

## Live-site drift, worth fixing at the source

Four inconsistencies between the published sites and this guide. None are urgent; all are cheap; each costs a little credibility where it sits.

| Where | What it says | What it should say |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| sogverse.sog.gg, About | "— The Principal of the School of Gaming" | "— The Princi-Pal" |
| sogverse.sog.gg, Make Gaming a Great Hobby | "a professional instructor who is there to guide and help" | "a Gedu who is there to guide and help" |
| sogverse.sog.gg, throughout | "clubs" used for both the group and the session | "club" for the group, "lesson" for the 90 minute session |
| sogverse.sog.gg, throughout | Minecraft presented as the whole offer | Minecraft, Roblox, Fortnite, and Rocket League, with the games framed as dimensions rather than the definition |

The last one is the most costly. The site currently reads as a Minecraft club business, which undersells a multi-game platform and will need rewriting again every time a title is added. Framing the games as dimensions of Sogverse makes the copy survive the next addition.
