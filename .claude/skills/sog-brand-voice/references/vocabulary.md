# Vocabulary

Casing and plurals are not cosmetic: inconsistent casing is the clearest signal that a brand
does not believe its own world.

Several of these terms name parts of the world that Sogverse does not show families yet
(on 2 Nov 2026 no family facing copy mentions Sogo, Toxies, Episodes, the store or the Gedu
Path). The form below is how to write the word; whether to write it is a separate question,
answered by whether the product has the thing. Describe a feature only if it exists.

**Search words we do not write.** A family searching "gaming classes" should find us, so
such words may be used as search keywords and in URLs; the copy still says "club".

**The free session**, the trial offer, is "Varaa maksuton kokeilukerta" in Finnish.

**Capitalising roles.** Brand copy uses lowercase gamer and parent, and the lowercase word
"level" for Gedu levels in running text. A compliance document such as the Safety Standard capitalises
Gamer, Parent and Educator where it defines them as parties with rights and obligations;
that is correct there and nowhere else. A capitalised Gamer on a home page reads as a
company describing a user category.

**Parents and Yty.** Where parents earn Yty, write it as a fact about them ("You earn Yty
too"), never as a favour to their child ("Help your child by earning Yty for them" turns a
parent into a resource).

## The Human Skills rule

**We call them Human Skills** (fi **ihmisyyden taidot**). Seven, in a fixed order:

> Creativity · Communication · Empathy · Critical thinking · Emotional intelligence ·
> Collaboration · Problem-solving

Never reorder them, abbreviate the list or add an eighth to suit a piece.

Each alternative fails in a specific way, which is how to defend the choice:

| Term | Why we reject it |
| :-- | :-- |
| Soft skills | Defines them by what they are not, and "soft" means weak. There is nothing soft about a nine year old holding their temper when a build collapses |
| 21st century skills | Coined to sound forward looking and now sounds like 2009 |
| Skills of the future | Defers the value to a date that never arrives. A parent wants to know what their child gets this year |
| Human Skills | Says what the skills are. The only one that will read the same in thirty years |

And, lightly and rarely: these are the skills that stay ours as more technical work is
automated.

**Human Skills and the Yty-Elements are one substance in two vocabularies.** The elements are
our internal architecture, inside the world; Human Skills is the same thing in the words a
parent, a school or a funder already has.

| Human Skill | Yty-Element |
| :-- | :-- |
| Emotional intelligence | Harmony |
| Empathy | Glow |
| Communication | Glow, and Valor in a team |
| Collaboration | Valor |
| Creativity | Valor |
| Critical thinking | Wit |
| Problem-solving | Wit |

Which to use follows the lore level. **Levels 0 and 1:** Human Skills, named plainly, no
Yty. **Level 2:** either, never both in one breath; connect them once and lightly ("Glow, our
name for empathy and communication"). **Level 3:** Yty-Elements only.

## The screen time rule

Our vision statement is "Where Screen Time Becomes Quality Time." That is not a breach of the
ban above, and seeing why is what lets a writer use the phrase. **Screen time may appear only
as the raw material our work transforms**, never as the problem, the enemy or a quantity to
cut.

| Ours | Not ours |
| :-- | :-- |
| Where screen time becomes quality time | Cut down on screen time |
| Turn screen time into something you are both glad about | Worried about too much screen time? |
| The screen time is happening anyway. This is what it could be | A healthy alternative to screen time |

The test: in your sentence, is screen time being transformed or fought? Transformed is ours.
Fought belongs to everyone else in this market. Quoting a parent who fights it is fine.

## The metaverse rule

"Metaverse" appears only as a joke we make about the industry, never as a description of
ourselves, at most once per piece: "Nowadays, someone might hype us as a metaverse. Whatever
that means...?" If a partner or a journalist uses the word first, accept it with the same
lightness and supply our own.

## The Discord rule

**Families and gamers never meet us on Discord; Sogverse is where our clubs happen**, and it
is built against the hazards open chat platforms carry: no direct messages, no strangers, no
outside invites. (Staff tooling is another matter: a Gedu may be reached on Discord about
substitutions. That is internal and is never how we describe the product.)

1. **Never call our own spaces Discord, a Discord server, or "our server".** They are
   Sogverse. A game server named as one is fine: "our own Minecraft server".
2. **Discord may be named in Level 0 and 1 copy only as the kind of thing we are an
   alternative to,** factually and without sneering. It is not built for eleven year olds and
   does not pretend to be.
3. **Never claim Discord is dangerous.** Say what we are, and let the comparison land.

> Right: Our clubs do not run on Discord or any open platform. They run in Sogverse, where
> there are no private messages and a Gedu is in every lesson.
>
> Wrong: Unlike dangerous platforms like Discord, where predators lurk, School of Gaming
> keeps your child safe.

The wrong one sells fear, which we never do to parents, and starts an argument about Discord
that distracts from what we built.

## Partners

**School of Gaming collaborates with Roblox and partners with Lynx Educate, and the two words
are never swapped.** "In collaboration with" is what Roblox asked for and the only phrasing
their name may appear in; "in partnership with" is Lynx's. Swapping them is a claim about a
legal relationship neither party agreed to. In every language: en "in collaboration with" /
"a collaboration between", fr "en collaboration avec" / "une collaboration entre", fi
"yhteistyössä", sv "i samarbete med". Klingon builds both on one root and is exempt. The rule
binds internal words too (component names, comments), because there the forbidden word sits
one copy and paste from a string. Copy about municipalities, schools and other partners says
"partnership" freely. Where a partner's logo may appear is a separate question of approvals,
in the app's `src/CLAUDE.md`.

**The two lawyer authored Roblox programme documents** (`robloxTerms`, `robloxPrivacy`) carry
Lynx's lawyer's text word for word, "course", "curriculum" and "facilitator" included,
because the wording is Lynx's and editing a signed text costs another legal review. Five
departures are sanctioned and no more: the tags that turn a cross reference into a link to
our own documents; the emphasis tag where the source bolds body text; the placeholder that
renders our support address; two sentences corrected to say how Sogverse works (a media
choice is changed on the child's own page in My SOG); and one sentence added under the
privacy policy's photography heading, with Lynx's agreement, saying a photograph from a
lesson may appear in the report every family in the group sees, where the media permission
was given. Anything further needs the owner. In those two namespaces Finnish and Swedish
follow the English with the plain category word (ohjaaja, ledare) where the English says
"facilitator"; French needs no split. `robloxSafeguarding` is ours and uses the house words
throughout.

## Fixed forms that are marks

"Sogverse" and "The Princi-Pal" stay untranslated in every language except that Finnish
calls him Reksi. The hyphen in Princi-Pal carries the joke; substituting a local word for a
headmaster deletes the joke and the character. Klingon is the deliberate exception that
renders "School of Gaming" as its own calque, and the About page's easter egg documents it;
a casing sweep must not "fix" it.
